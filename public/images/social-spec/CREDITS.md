# Social spec post credits

Four spec Instagram feed posts (1080x1350, 4:5) for brands Angel breaks down on the blog: two Ghia, one Fishwife, one Graza. Spec concepts, not affiliated with any of these brands. Data, captions, and strategy: `lib/social-spec.ts`. Strategy notes and research: `.harness/reports/social-spec-strategy.md`. Shown by `components/SocialSpecRow.tsx`.

Editable source: `.harness/source/social-spec/posts.html` (one post per `?p=` id: ga = ghia-1, g2 = ghia-2, f1 = fishwife-1, z1 = graza-1; gb is the unused second version of ghia-1). `render.sh` renders the PNGs with headless Chrome; the JPGs here are those PNGs saved at 300KB or less. Figma file https://www.figma.com/design/fVpD1JrzJ0zaTXmymFwSU2, page "social spec", holds the same four renders as flat image frames (ids in `lib/social-spec.ts`); the v1 frames sit in a section marked retired.

No brand logos, product, bottle, tin, or campaign photos are used, and nothing from Pinterest or Behance. No AI-generated images. Brand names are set as live type in close Google Fonts: Smythe and Archivo (Ghia), Fraunces and Albert Sans (Fishwife), Libre Caslon Condensed, Courier Prime, and DM Sans (Graza).

Photos are from Unsplash, free to use under the Unsplash License (unsplash.com/license). Attribution is not required. They are credited here anyway. Each was checked on 2026-10-12 as a free photo, not Unsplash+.

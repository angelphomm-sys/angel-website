Before college there was a production line.

From July 2019 to November 2020 I worked full-time at Marvin, the window and door manufacturer in Warroad, Minnesota, up near the Canadian border. Daily production targets, quality targets, safety glasses, a badge. My mom worked there too. That's us in the photo, on the floor.

This fall Utah Valley University (UVU) chose me as the featured student in its Welcome Weeks convocation video, the one shown to the entire incoming freshman class, because my path into higher education wasn't the usual one. Here's the version that fits in a blog post.

## What the floor taught me

**Showing up is most of the job.** A line doesn't run if people don't come in. I learned to be the person who came in, and later, the person who trained the new hires on safety and quality standards so the line kept running when I wasn't on it.

**Quality is a habit, not an inspection.** You don't catch a bad unit at the end. You build it right the first time, every time, because someone downstream is counting on you. Every marketing deliverable I've shipped since gets the same treatment: check it before it leaves.

**Fast environments reward calm people.** The floor was loud and the targets were daily. The people who did well were the ones who didn't panic. That's the same skill as the hour before doors open at an event.

## Getting to UVU

I moved to Utah and enrolled at Utah Valley University's Woodbury School of Business as a first-generation college student, with support from TRIO Student Support Services and the First Generation Center. In 2024 I became one of the inaugural Hope Scholars, a scholarship program I now help market from the inside, and in August 2026 I finished an Associate in Business Management with honors on the way to a bachelor's in digital marketing.

Along the way I kept working: serving and then leading the floor at a sushi restaurant, a marketing internship at Shyft, a brand-and-YouTube internship at Yoodlize, and a photography business I'd been running since 2018.

## Why this is on a marketing portfolio

Because the job I want, building live moments for consumer, sports, and entertainment brands, is production work. There's a room, a clock, and a hundred details that have to be right at the same time. I've done that. I did it in safety glasses first.

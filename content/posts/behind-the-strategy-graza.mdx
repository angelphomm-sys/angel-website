Graza's marketing strategy turned olive oil into something people film. It launched in January 2022 with a green squeeze bottle built for the camera, free oil for food creators, and online sales before any grocery shelf. It took retail only after demand showed up. If you are launching a food product, the order of those moves is the part to copy.

Graza's own TikTok from June 2023 runs the caption "so fresh, squeezy, and clean."

<TikTok url="https://www.tiktok.com/@getgraza/video/7247166475998563630" title="Graza squeeze bottle TikTok, June 2023" />

During launch week, cookbook author Molly Baz posted the oil to her story. "The second she posted that on her story, it was a bloodbath," co-founder Andrew Benin told [Klaviyo](https://www.klaviyo.com/blog/guerrilla-marketing-for-ecommerce). Graza had not paid her. It had sent free oil with no posting requirement.

## How did Graza become so popular?

Graza sells two oils with the job in the name. Drizzle is a 500 ml finishing oil, and Sizzle is a 750 ml cooking oil. They launched at $20 and $15, per a 2026 [Tasting Table story](https://www.tastingtable.com/2194996/why-graza-olive-oil-popular/). Benin told FoodNavigator in 2022 that Graza is "non-heritage, non-luxury." It sold oil for eggs on a Tuesday.

The launch ran on three moves, per a 2023 Klaviyo case study. The team gifted about 25 packs a week to food creators, with no strings attached. It built an email list of about 2,000 people before launch, and it sold online first. Bloom Nutrition started from the other side: its founder was the creator, as I cover in [Bloom's community playbook](/blog/bloom-nutrition-marketing-strategy).

By April 2022, Graza had passed $500,000 in revenue, per Klaviyo. By the end of 2024, Inside Retail Asia estimated revenue at $48.4 million.

## Why did Graza use a squeeze bottle?

The squeeze bottle was a content decision. "We own the molds," Benin told FoodNavigator. He told [AIGA's Eye on Design](https://eyeondesign.aiga.org/how-to-build-an-entire-brand-identity-around-a-squeeze-bottle/) in 2022 that the form factor "kind of does the work for us."

On a phone screen, a glass bottle glugs and a squeeze bottle draws a line. Creators who cooked with Graza squeezed the same bottle on camera, so each video showed the product doing its job.

## How does the Graza marketing strategy flip a grocery launch?

The Graza marketing strategy ran the usual grocery launch in reverse.

### Don't: pay for the shelf first

- Pay slotting fees, which a 2003 FTC staff report found "can make up a large fraction" of some products' first-year revenue.
- Buy shelf space before a single shopper has tried the product.

### Do: earn the shelf

- Sell online first. Graza made $100,000 in week one, per Klaviyo.
- Seed creators with no required posts.
- Take retail after demand shows up. Whole Foods carried Graza nationwide by the end of its first year, per Inside Retail Asia.
- Buy TV last. Graza launched "Seriously Serious," its largest paid campaign to date, in March 2026 to sell its new olive oil mayo and aioli, per [Marketing Dive](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/graza-olive-oil-enters-new-category-with-largest-paid-campaign-to-date/813675/).

In October 2023, Modern Retail reported a projected 7,500 stores by year-end. Marketing Dive counted more than 28,000 locations by March 2026.

## Where does Graza's model strain at scale?

Copying comes first. California Olive Ranch built its own squeeze bottle after customers asked for one, [Modern Retail reported in April 2024](https://www.modernretail.co/operations/to-squeeze-or-not-to-squeeze-the-olive-oil-world-is-divided-over-plastic-packaging/). Plastic comes next: Kosterina founder Katina Mountanos told Modern Retail, "Olive oil should never be packaged in plastic." Graza added glass bottles in August 2025, per The Dieline.

Cost is the third limit. In October 2023, Modern Retail reported that Graza was paying more than double its early 2022 import costs and had raised its price from $15 to $16 a bottle. Reach is the fourth. At 28,000 locations, most shoppers meet the bottle on a shelf, not in a video.

## What I would have done differently

I would have moved the ownable parts off the mold sooner. The illustrated labels and the Drizzle and Sizzle names are harder to copy than a bottle. The 2026 mayo and aioli launch tests whether the name can carry a product that is not olive oil. I would also have offered glass in year two, as a choice, before critics asked for it.

## How to apply Graza's marketing strategy to your launch

- **Design the on-camera moment first.** Decide what the product does in a 10-second video, then design the package around it.
- **Seed without strings.** Graza sent about 25 packs a week to food creators and asked for nothing back.
- **Prove demand online, then pitch retail.** A sold-out first week is a stronger pitch than a slotting fee.
- **Own what a mold cannot copy.** Names, illustration, and voice outlast a bottle shape.

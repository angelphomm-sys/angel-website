You would expect a tinned fish brand to start with the fish. Fishwife's marketing strategy started with the tin. Food Dive reported that founder Becca Millstein hired an illustrator and built the brand identity before she sourced the product. Five decisions grew from that order: the tin, the name, the ethics, social, and drops. Each transfers to a small food brand with no ad budget.

## Why is the tin the center of Fishwife's marketing strategy?

Millstein told [Food Dive](https://www.fooddive.com/news/fishwife-success-tinned-fish/726922/) in September 2024 that she had already hired illustrator Danny Miller and "created the initial brand identity." Food Dive credited the colorful packaging with setting Fishwife apart in a category European brands long owned. A tin that photographs well turns customer posts into free media.

What I would change: a busy tin makes one can beautiful and a full shelf noisy. In April 2024, Fortune reported about $6 million in annual revenue and 1,800 retail locations. Millstein told [Inside Retail](https://insideretail.com.au/sectors/fishwifes-becca-millstein-talks-brand-journey-social-media-and-subscriptions-202501) in January 2025 that Fishwife was in 5,000 brick-and-mortar retailers, and [Sporked reported](https://sporked.com/article/fishwife-tinned-fish-costco-news/) a national Costco rollout in early 2026. I would add one fixed band that holds a row together from six feet away.

## Why is it called Fishwife?

"Fishwife" was once a gendered insult aimed at the daughters and wives of 16th-century fishermen, [Secret NYC reported](https://secretnyc.co/fishwife-nyc-pop-up/) in October 2024. The brand took the word back. Product names do the other job. Smoked Rainbow Trout with Red Chimichurri says exactly what is in the can.

What I would change: the story behind the name lives in press coverage. I would print one line of it on the tin.

## Why doesn't Fishwife lead with sustainability?

Fishwife's [Our Story page](https://eatfishwife.com/pages/our-story) lists certified fisheries and names its canneries, from Santoña in Spain to a micro cannery outside Copenhagen. Millstein told [Marketing Brew](https://www.marketingbrew.com/stories/2022/04/07/fishwife-made-its-presence-known-organically-now-it-s-thinking-bigger) in April 2022 that responsible sourcing was "table stakes these days." She said the brand's primary strategy was messaging "deliciousness, humor, exuberance, artfulness" instead.

I would test one campaign built around the Santoña cannery. A named place travels further than an adjective.

## Did Fishwife use paid advertising?

Not at first. Marketing Brew reported in April 2022 that Fishwife had "not done any paid marketing." Millstein ran every social channel herself for two years, with one rule: "speak to our audience like I'd speak to my best friend."

In September 2023, Fishwife told Help Scout that 40 percent of customers found the brand through social. The #tinnedfishdatenight hashtag had 7.4 million TikTok views by September 2022, [Bustle reported](https://www.bustle.com/life/tinned-fish-date-night-tiktok-trend), naming Fishwife among the most popular brands. On Shark Tank, Millstein took $350,000 from Lori Greiner and Candace Nelson for 6 percent plus advisory shares, Tasting Table reported in January 2024.

The risk I see: founder voice does not transfer. Co-founder Caroline Goldfarb has since left, Food Dive reported, so the voice rests on one person. I would have written it down while Millstein still ran every channel.

## Why does Fishwife run drops instead of launches?

A dated drop gives people a reason to buy this week. Fishwife runs its collaborations that way. Tasting Table reported the Fly By Jing chili crisp salmon in March 2022. Chaco announced a Fishwife sandal in a May 2025 press release.

Spoon University covered the Whole Foods totes released in July 2026. Sweetgreen's Business Wire release set a Summer Niçoise for August 11 to 24, 2026, with a co-branded tuna tin as the product.

Where I would push: the Sweetgreen tin is the smartest drop, because opening it is the product experience. The Chaco sandal only moves merchandise. I would make every drop put a tin in someone's hands.

## What to copy from Fishwife's marketing strategy

1. **Build the identity before the product.** Millstein hired an illustrator before she sourced the fish.
2. **Design the package as media.** A tin that photographs well turns every customer post into free reach.
3. **Write the voice down early.** One founder ran every channel for two years. A written voice guide lets a second person run it.
4. **Make every drop put the product in someone's hands.** The Sweetgreen tin did. The sandal did not.

I would test one weekly slot with a fixed format, owned by a named person who is not the founder.

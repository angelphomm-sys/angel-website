Every semester the Hope Scholars program at the Utah Valley University (UVU) Woodbury School of Business needs to show two audiences what the scholarship actually does: the donors who fund it and the students who might apply. A wall of text doesn't do that. A carousel does.

This one covers my summer, the Italy entrepreneurship study abroad, a convocation video shoot, a new internship, and the back-to-school potluck, but the format is the point. Here's how it's built.

## The brief

- **Audience:** donors and program council members first, prospective scholars second.
- **Goal:** make the program's impact feel personal, not institutional.
- **Format:** Instagram carousel, 4:5, five slides, scrapbook aesthetic that matches the program's warm, hand-made feel.
- **Voice:** first person, short sentences, one idea per slide.

**1 · The hook.** A cover has one job: earn the swipe. Two photos, a clip, a torn-paper texture, and four words. The subtitle does the work: *a summer of growth, lessons, and new perspectives.*

**2 · The highlights.** Three moments, three labels: took on new opportunities, created impact, embraced new spaces. Each label is a claim the rest of the carousel backs up. The convocation-video still shows the program's reach; the group selfie shows the community.

**3 · The story.** One lesson, told properly. On the study abroad I watched a glassblower in Venice work at his own pace, no rush, no shortcuts, and still build something people traveled across the world to see. *Pace over pressure* became the line. This is the slide people screenshot.

**4 · Behind the camera.** The program funded projects that put me on both sides of the lens. Showing the crew and the camera makes "impact" concrete for a donor: this is what your money did.

**5 · The close.** Onward into senior year, and a photo with the people who make the program run. It ends on gratitude, which is what a donor audience wants to feel.

## What made it work

- **Consistency over decoration.** Same paper texture, same green accent, same photo treatment on every slide. The scrapbook look reads as intentional because it repeats.
- **One line per slide.** If a slide needs a paragraph, it's two slides.
- **Real photos only.** Nothing from a stock library. The credibility of a recap is that it happened.

## What I'd change

- **Add a slide with one number.** The program's total scholars or dollars raised, for donors who skim.

On a Saturday in April, the first fan in line outside Carpet Company's new Baltimore store showed up before 3 a.m., about eight hours before the doors opened. The best brand activations of 2026 worked the same way: they gave people a job (collect a bead, post a selfie for your state, get in line early) and left public proof that a crowd came. I picked eight from February to September that pass that test, and I'll name two clever ideas that don't.

A brand activation is a live moment a brand stages so people do something with it, in person or online. The eight below run in date order, and each entry covers what happened, the proof that people came, and the move you can reuse.

**The short version**

- The strongest activations named a verb (collect, trade, line up, post) before they picked a venue.
- Most sent people home with an object, which became both the proof they were there and the thing they posted.
- Smaller beat bigger more than once: Seattle's 400-drone scoreboard gave fans more to share than a 2,500-drone show.
- Decide what you will count before you build. The public proof for this whole list is turnout, and turnout is the easy part.

## The 8 best brand activations of 2026, with proof people came

### 01 · Levi's: Home Turf at Super Bowl LX

*Retail pop-up · February 5 to 7 · San Francisco*

When Super Bowl LX came to Levi's Stadium, Levi's threw its own party in San Francisco, where the company is based. Its Home Turf pop-up took over the One Montgomery building in the Financial District from Thursday to Saturday of game week, per the [brand's January 26 announcement](https://www.levistrauss.com/2026/01/26/levis-home-turf/). On February 6, NPR rebuilt its Tiny Desk set there for a live show, the first time it had recreated the set with a live performance, per [NPR's February 4 announcement](https://www.npr.org/2026/02/04/g-s1-108570/npr-music-and-levis-announce-tiny-desk-experience-at-super-bowl-lx).

<Figure src="/images/blog/10-brand-moments-people-showed-up-for-2026/levis-home-turf-pop-up-graphic.jpg" alt="Levi's Home Turf pop-up graphic in red spray paint on black, with the Levi's batwing logo, 1 Montgomery Street, San Francisco, and February 5-7, 2026" caption="The address and the three dates sit in the corners, so the pop-up reads as an event with a start and an end. Image: Levi's." ratio="16/9" />

More than a thousand visitors a day came through, per [Retail Brew](https://www.retailbrew.com/stories/2026/02/09/why-levi-s-made-a-big-play-for-the-super-bowl) in February 2026. The move to copy: when a big event lands in your city, host the locals on your own turf instead of fighting for space in the stadium crowd.

### 02 · Harry Styles: 16 album pop-ups

*Entertainment · March 6 · 16 cities worldwide*

Harry Styles released "Kiss All the Time. Disco, Occasionally." on March 6 and opened 16 pop-up shops around the world the same day, including one in Williamsburg, Brooklyn, [NBC New York reported](https://www.nbcnewyork.com/entertainment/harry-styles-nyc-pop-up-revealed/6472774). His team announced the shops on Instagram that week.

<Instagram url="https://www.instagram.com/p/DVdxnatDZCw/" title="HSHQ on Instagram announcing the KATTDO pop-ups, March 2026" />

In Atlanta, hundreds of fans lined up along the Beltline on opening day, GPB News reported in March 2026 in a story [republished by Rough Draft Atlanta](https://roughdraftatlanta.com/2026/03/06/harry-styles-atlanta-pop-up/). An album is something people stream alone, so a pop-up gives them a reason to find each other. Brands can borrow the format too, and I broke down how [Bloom Nutrition turned its NYC market pop-up into content](/blog/bloom-nutrition-marketing-strategy). The move to copy: give a private moment a physical address for a few days.

### 03 · Cheez-It: Kon's Double Double bundle

*CPG drop · March 26 · cheezit.com*

Cheez-It put Charlotte Hornets rookie Kon Knueppel on a limited bundle of his two favorite flavors, White Cheddar and Extra Toasty, sold only on cheezit.com. The [brand's March 26 release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cheez-it-and-basketball-star-kon-knueppel-team-up-for-limited-edition-double-double-flavor-bundle-302725454.html) named both drop dates up front, March 26 and April 6. It is the one online entry on this list, and I kept it because the line formed at checkout.

<Figure src="/images/blog/10-brand-moments-people-showed-up-for-2026/cheez-it-kons-double-double-bundle.jpg" alt="A red Cheez-It box with Kon Knueppel holding a basketball and the words Kon's Double Double Bundle, surrounded by flying crackers on a basketball court" caption="The box is built like a trading card: his photo, his signature, and a name only fans would get. Image: Cheez-It." ratio="16/9" />

The first drop sold out as soon as it went on sale, [Distractify reported](https://www.distractify.com/p/kon-knueppel-cheez-its-where-to-buy) the next day, at $20 a bundle. The move to copy: tie a limited drop to a person fans already follow, and announce the second date with the first so people who miss out still have a reason to come back.

### 04 · Carpet Company: the Baltimore flagship line

*Retail opening · April 11 · Baltimore*

Carpet Company, a Baltimore streetwear brand, opened its flagship on North Avenue in the Station North neighborhood on Saturday, April 11. Doors opened at 11 a.m.

<Figure src="/images/blog/10-brand-moments-people-showed-up-for-2026/carpet-company-baltimore-flagship-interior.jpg" alt="Inside the Carpet Company flagship: red ceilings, a large red and chrome pyramid, a green Carpet sign, and jackets hung along a wavy rail" caption="The store is a set as much as a shop, which gives a line something to photograph once it gets inside. Image: Carpet Company." ratio="16/9" />

The first fans in line arrived before 3 a.m., and hundreds more wrapped around the block, [The Baltimore Banner reported](https://www.thebanner.com/culture/carpet-company-opening-baltimore-hundreds-line-JAMVFAA2UZFWBACJZIFA7OOCE4) that afternoon. Fans drove in from Northern Virginia and Newark, New Jersey. A local brand's first store gave its fans a hometown occasion, which is the same instinct Levi's followed in San Francisco on a much bigger budget. The move to copy: treat an opening like an event with a start time, because a start time is what makes a line.

### 05 · Bank of America: BofA Fan Bands

*World Cup · from June 11 · 11 U.S. host cities*

Bank of America built free World Cup bracelets from a set of 140 collectible beads and started handing them out across the 11 U.S. host cities on June 11, with more than 2 million bands planned, per its [June 9 release](https://newsroom.bankofamerica.com/content/newsroom/press-releases/2026/06/bofa-kicks-off-fifa-world-cup-2026--with-2-million-free-fan-band.html). As the tournament moved on, the bank added new beads for the Round of 16, quarterfinals, semifinals, and final.

<Figure src="/images/blog/10-brand-moments-people-showed-up-for-2026/bofa-fan-band-nynj-final-bead.jpg" alt="A black braided BofA Fan Band with a Statue of Liberty bead, a pizza slice, a 2026 Final bead, a yellow taxi, and a red apple" caption="Each bead marks a city or a round, so a full band is a record of where you went and when. Image: Bank of America." ratio="900/452" />

Event Marketer described hours-long lines in its [June 26 roundup](https://www.eventmarketer.com/article/fifa-world-cup-2026-brand-activations-roundup/), and the bank said it had given away more than 700,000 Fan Bands in the first two weeks, per its [June 29 release](https://newsroom.bankofamerica.com/content/newsroom/press-releases/2026/06/bank-of-america-debuts-new-knockout-round-beads-for-free-in-dema.html). The knockout beads were the smart part, because they gave fans a reason to come back after their first visit. The move to copy: build a collection that grows on a schedule, so one visit turns into several.

### 06 · Joyride: the 50 States Challenge

*World record · June 13 · Instagram and YouTube*

Joyride, the candy brand YouTuber Ryan Trahan co-owns, per the [Path to Purchase Institute](https://p2pi.com/how-joyride-broke-guinness-world-record-target) in 2025, gave fans 24 hours on June 13 to post a selfie with a Joyride bag, geotagged to their state, while a livestream ran on YouTube. The winning state got its own candy.

Fans posted 11,495 approved photos, enough for a Guinness World Records title, and the livestream drew more than 2.6 million views, [Mass Market Retailers reported](https://massmarketretailers.com/joyride-just-broke-a-guinness-world-record-again-and-named-americas-no-1-candy-state/) in June 2026. Idaho won, and its prize was Potato Wedges, a huckleberry-flavored gummy. Utah, where I go to school, finished third on [Joyride's leaderboard](https://www.joyride50stateschallenge.com/).

<Figure src="/images/blog/10-brand-moments-people-showed-up-for-2026/joyride-idaho-potato-wedges.jpg" alt="A yellow Joyride bag of Potato Wedges gummies in huckleberry flavor, marked Idaho and Joyride Day 2026" caption="The prize carries the winning state's name and the event's name on the bag, so the trophy keeps advertising the contest. Image: Joyride." ratio="1/1" />

The move to copy: let people compete as a team for a shared reward, since nobody wants their state to lose.

### 07 · Visit Seattle: the drone scoreboard

*World Cup · June 15 to July 6 · Seattle Center*

Visit Seattle flew 400 drones by the Space Needle after each of the city's six World Cup matches. Each free 12-minute show displayed both teams' final score with their country's flag, and thousands of visiting fans and residents gathered to watch, per the tourism group's [June 22 release](https://visitseattle.org/press/press-releases/visit-seattle-drone-scoreboard-world-cup/).

<Figure src="/images/blog/10-brand-moments-people-showed-up-for-2026/visit-seattle-drone-scoreboard-crowd.jpg" alt="Fans in Bosnia and Herzegovina jerseys photograph a drone scoreboard showing two flags and a 3-1 score beside the Space Needle at night" caption="Look at the man with the camera: the score in the sky is the souvenir. Image: Visit Seattle." ratio="3/2" />

That same month, Michelob ULTRA put on a show with more than 2,500 drones at a VIP event in Santa Monica, Event Marketer reported in its June 26 roundup. Seattle's show was about a sixth the size, yet it is the one I'd copy, because it put news in the sky: the score of the match people had just watched. The move to copy: show a crowd something about its own day, and people will photograph it.

### 08 · Buddy: the orange unicorn in the wild

*Film launch · summer · Los Angeles, New York, San Diego*

Before the horror film Buddy opened on August 28, its orange unicorn caused chaos in movie theaters, led a "Killer Fitness" workout class, offered free hugs near the Santa Monica Pier, and turned up at San Diego Comic-Con, [Event Marketer reported](https://www.eventmarketer.com/article/film-tv-marketing-stunts-summer-2026/) in September 2026.

<TikTok url="https://www.tiktok.com/@buddy.the.unicorn/video/7675050369164070175" title="Buddy the Unicorn free hugs at the Santa Monica Pier on TikTok, August 2026" />

The Buddy TikTok account neared 1 million followers, and its most viral video hit 10.9 million views, per the same report. The film opened to $5.3 million and then grew to $5.7 million in its second weekend, [TheWrap reported](https://www.thewrap.com/industry-news/box-office/buddy-horror-film-indie-box-office-hit/) in September 2026, which is what word of mouth looks like at the box office. A character people want to follow also powers [Duolingo's social media strategy](/blog/duolingo-social-media-strategy). The move to copy: give your mascot a personality and send it somewhere it doesn't belong.

## What did the best brand activations of 2026 have in common?

The best brand activations of 2026 gave people a verb: collect, trade, compete, line up, post. The verb came first, and the venue, the drones, and the famous face came second.

Most of the eight also sent people home with something, whether a bracelet, a bag of merch, or a photo of the score in the sky. The object is proof you were there, and proof is what gets posted.

People were ready to show up this year. More than 9,017,000 people attended FIFA Fan Festivals across 13 host cities, the highest figure since 2006, per [FIFA's July 27 release](https://inside.fifa.com/organisation/media-releases/fan-festival-extends-historic-world-cup-2026-experience-millions-record-breaking-celebrations-host-countries). Even before kickoff, [Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com/blog/press/newsroom/world-cup-fever-goes-irl/) counted U.S. World Cup events up 403 percent over the same stretch before the 2022 tournament, with U.S. attendance up 572 percent.

My position: in 2026, participation beat spectacle. The usual read is that the biggest budget wins, but a 400-drone scoreboard, a cracker box, and a pre-dawn line at a local shop drew people because each one gave them something to do or something to prove.

## What to take from the best brand activations of 2026

1. **Name the verb before the venue.** What people will do is the part they describe to a friend afterward.
2. **Send people home with proof.** A bead, a bag, or a photo of the sky gives each guest a reason to post, and each post reaches people who weren't there.
3. **Plan the second visit on day one.** Bank of America's knockout beads and Cheez-It's second drop date gave anyone who missed round one a reason to return.
4. **Borrow a moment, then make it local.** Levi's, Carpet Company, and Visit Seattle each hosted their own neighborhood instead of competing for the stadium crowd.
5. **Pick the number before you build.** Bank of America counted bands and Joyride counted approved selfies, so both had a result to announce within weeks.

## How do you measure a brand activation?

Count what people did, and decide what you will count before you build. Two of the year's cleverest ideas are missing from this list for that reason.

Stella Artois offered up to $100,000 in reimbursements to fans 21 and older who bought a Stella at a bar during weekday World Cup matches and filed the receipt as an "expense report," [The Source reported](https://thesource.com/2026/06/03/stella-artois-work-from-bar-fifa-world-cup-2026/) in June 2026. McDonald's toured a confessional booth built from its ordering kiosk through four cities so people could share first-job stories, [Marketing Dive reported](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/mcdonalds-reality-tv-inspired-campaign-turns-kiosks-into-confessionals/813871/) in March 2026. I could not find a published count for either one, so both may have worked, but neither has shown it in public.

## The catch: a crowd is the easy part to prove

The proof I used is thinner than the list makes it look. Almost every number above is turnout: a line, a sellout, a count of bands or selfies on the day. Turnout tells you people came, but it does not tell you whether they bought more jeans, opened a bank account, or thought about Cheez-It again a month later.

Some of these counts also measure demand for something free. Bank of America planned more than 2 million bands and gave away more than 700,000 in two weeks, which is a strong giveaway number that says little about how fans felt about the bank. Buddy shows the gap from the other side: one TikTok passed 10.9 million views, and the film opened to $5.3 million. A view costs nothing and a ticket costs money, so a viral video is a weak stand-in for a sale.

I also built in a selection problem. I only kept activations with a public count, so the list favors brands that publish results, and Stella Artois or McDonald's may have outperformed half these entries without saying so.

I still think participation beat spectacle this year, but the next step is to count the second action, the one that happens after the line. I write the verb before I book the venue, and I used that rule on a concept for [Pi Mai Lao, the Lao New Year](/case/embracing-our-story): one free celebration a city plans its weekend around. It is also the question I'm bringing into my new role in Marketing and Fan Experience with UVU Athletics.

If you plan an event this year, write one sentence first: "People will (verb), and afterward we will report (number)." Then add a second number for what they do a month later, and test whether the event moved it.

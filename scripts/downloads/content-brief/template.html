01Target audience and goal

Target audienceWho it's for and what they already know.e.g. Marketers starting out who write their own posts
Funnel stageLearning, comparing, or ready to act?☐ Awareness ☐ Consideration ☐ Decision
Content goalWhat the post should do for the reader and for you.e.g. Readers write a brief; I get template downloads

02Keyword research

Free tools: Ahrefs Keyword Generator, Google autocomplete, People also ask.

Primary keywordInclude monthly volume and keyword difficulty (KD).e.g. seo content brief (500 a month, KD 1)
Secondary keywordsTwo or three related phrases.e.g. seo content brief template, content brief template
Search intentWhat kind of page ranks for it?e.g. Informational: how-to guides with templates☐ Informational ☐ Commercial ☐ Transactional
Questions to answerFrom People also ask. Use them as headings and FAQs.e.g. What is an SEO content brief? What should it include?

04Content angle

Content gapWhat the top results don't cover.e.g. Every guide is written for agency teams
Unique valueA story, result, or example from your own work.e.g. My own filled-in brief and what I'd change
Editorial angleYour post's main idea in one sentence.e.g. A plain-language brief for people who write their own posts
Target word countThe top-three average ÷ 3 is a starting point.e.g. 900 to 1,200

05On-page SEO

SEO title50 to 60 characters, primary keyword first.e.g. How to Write an SEO Content Brief (With a Free Template)
Meta description150 to 160 characters.e.g. How to write an SEO content brief in seven plain steps, plus a free template you can download.
URL slugShort, lowercase, hyphens.e.g. /blog/how-to-write-an-seo-content-brief
Ready to write? You can name the audience, search intent, and content gap in one sentence each.

09Brand voice and guidelines

Voice and tonee.g. Plain, friendly, first person
Must includeDisclaimers, required terms, product names.e.g. A credit line for every image
AvoidClaims, words, or topics to leave out.e.g. Numbers without a source

10Pre-publish checklist

Primary keyword in the title, first 100 words, and one H2
Title and meta description match section 05
Every number links to a source
Two or three internal links
Images credited, with alt text
CTA matches the content goal
KPI check on your calendar for 30 days out

Example: a filled-in brief

The brief behind “Owala vs. Stanley: New Colors or New Products?” Search data from Ahrefs (US). Word counts are from the finished post.

Content typeComparison, new blog post
Target audiencePeople starting out in marketing who want to see how two brands grow after one hit product
Funnel stageAwareness
Content goalReaders understand the tradeoff between refreshing a best-seller and launching new products
Primary keywordowala vs stanley (1,800 a month, easy to rank for)
Secondary keywordsowala color drop (4,600), why is owala so popular (250), stanley marketing strategy
Search intentCommercial: every top result is a product test
Questions to answerIs Owala more popular than Stanley? What is an Owala Color Drop?
Content gapNo one compared the two brands’ marketing
Unique valueMy side-by-side read of their products, ads, and blogs
Editorial angleNew colors or new products: what each strategy costs and when it works
Target word count≈470 suggested; finished at 1,480 to cover products, ads, and blogs
SEO titleOwala vs. Stanley: New Colors or New Products?
Meta descriptionOwala sells the same FreeSip in a new color every month. Stanley is building bags, coolers, and soccer collections around the Quencher. What each costs.
URL slug/blog/owala-vs-stanley
Call to actionNotice where each brand’s ads and links send you: refresh it or build something new?
KPIGoogle ranking for “owala vs stanley” and time on page

Content brief

One page of decisions before the first draft. Fill it in order; most briefs take about 20 minutes.

01Reader and goal

ReaderWho is this for? One sentence, specific enough to picture.
GoalWhat should they know or do when they finish?

02Search

Primary keywordThe phrase your reader types. Add monthly searches and difficulty.
Secondary keywordsTwo or three related phrases or questions (People also ask, autocomplete).
Search intentSearch it yourself. What do the results show people expect: a how-to, a list, a review?

03Angle and shape

The gapWhat none of the top three results do. Fill in page 2 first, then summarize here.
AngleThe one idea your piece adds.
LengthTop-three average ÷ 3 = a starting point, not a quota.

04Next step and proof

Call to actionThe one thing the reader should do next. Decide it now, not after the draft.
Success measureOne number to check 30 days after publishing (ranking, time on page, clicks).
Ready to write? You should be able to say the reader, the goal, and the gap in one sentence each. If you can't, keep researching before you draft.

Example: a filled-in brief

The brief behind “Owala vs. Stanley: New Colors or New Products?” on angelphommachan.com. Keyword data from Ahrefs, US.

ReaderMarketers and marketing students who want to see how two brands grow after one hit product
GoalUnderstand the tradeoff between refreshing a best-seller and launching new products
Primary keywordowala vs stanley (1,800 monthly searches, very low difficulty)
Secondary keywordsowala color drop (4,600), why is owala so popular (250), stanley marketing strategy
Search intentShoppers comparing the bottles: every top result is a product test
Top three resultsReviewed, BYU Design Review, and a blogger review, about 1,000 to 1,600 words each
The gapNo one compared the two brands’ marketing strategies
AngleNew colors or new products: what each strategy costs and when it works
LengthAverage ÷ 3 ≈ 470 words; finished at about 1,480 to cover the products, ads, and blogs
SourcesRetail Dive, Fast Company, The Drum, Owala’s Color Drop FAQ, ASI (each opened and checked)
Call to actionNotice where each brand’s ads and links send you, then answer: refresh it or build something new?
Success measureRanking for “owala vs stanley” and time on page after 30 days
What changed after research: One planned quote was no longer on Owala’s site, so it came out before drafting. A classmate pointed out that the main growth number came from a corporate-gifting search platform, so the post now says so plainly.

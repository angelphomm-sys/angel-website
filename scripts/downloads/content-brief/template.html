01Reader and goal

ReaderWho is this for? Specific enough to picture.e.g. Marketers starting out who write their own blog posts
GoalWhat should they know or be able to do when they finish?e.g. Write a one-page brief before their next post

02Search

Free tools work: the Ahrefs Keyword Generator, Google's autocomplete, and the "People also ask" box.

Main keywordThe phrase your reader types into Google. Add monthly searches and difficulty if your tool shows them.e.g. seo content brief (500 searches a month, easy)
Related searchesTwo or three other phrases or questions people search.e.g. seo content brief template, content brief template
What searchers wantSearch the main keyword. What kind of page shows up most: a how-to, a list, a review?e.g. Step-by-step guides, most with a template

03What already shows up on Google

04Your angle

What's missingWhat none of the top three do. Use section 03.e.g. Every guide is written for agency teams, not people writing their own posts
What only you can addA story, photo, result, or example from your own work.e.g. My own filled-in brief and what I'd change
Your angleThe one idea your post adds, in one sentence.e.g. A plain-language brief for people who write their own posts
LengthA range. Average the top three and divide by 3 for a starting point, not a rule.e.g. 900 to 1,200 words

05Title and Google listing

This is what people see in search results before they click.

Page titleMain keyword near the start. About 60 characters or fewer so it isn't cut off.e.g. How to Write an SEO Content Brief (With a Free Template)
Meta descriptionThe short description under your title on Google. About 150 characters. Google may rewrite it.e.g. How to write an SEO content brief in seven plain steps, what has changed in search and what hasn't, plus a free template you can download.
Web address (URL)Short, lowercase, words separated by hyphens.e.g. /blog/how-to-write-an-seo-content-brief
Ready to write? You should be able to say the reader, the goal, and what's missing in one sentence each. If you can't, keep researching before you draft.

08Next step and proof

Next step for the readerThe one thing they should do after reading (your call to action), plus the link.e.g. Download the template
How you'll know it workedOne number to check 30 days after publishing.e.g. Template downloads and Google ranking

09Notes for the writer

Skip this if you're writing it yourself, or use it to remember the rules you set.

VoiceHow it should sound.e.g. Plain and friendly, first person
Must includeRequired lines, disclaimers, product names, or terms.e.g. A credit line for every photo
AvoidClaims, words, or topics to leave out.e.g. Any number I can't link to a source

10Before you publish

Main keyword in the title, the first 100 words, and one section heading
Every number links to a source you opened

Photos credited, each with a short description for screen readers (alt text)
Page title and meta description match section 05
The next step matches the goal in section 01

Example: a filled-in brief

The brief behind “Owala vs. Stanley: New Colors or New Products?” on angelphommachan.com. Search numbers from Ahrefs, US. Section word counts are from the finished post.

Where it's postedBlog on angelphommachan.com (new post)
ReaderPeople starting out in marketing who want to see how two brands grow after one hit product
GoalUnderstand the tradeoff between refreshing a best-seller and launching new products
Main keywordowala vs stanley (1,800 searches a month, easy to rank for)
Related searchesowala color drop (4,600), why is owala so popular (250), stanley marketing strategy
What searchers wantShoppers comparing the bottles: every top result is a product test
What's missingNo one compared the two brands’ marketing
What only I can addMy own side-by-side read of their products, ads, and blogs
AngleNew colors or new products: what each strategy costs and when it works
LengthAverage ÷ 3 ≈ 470 words; finished at about 1,480 to cover products, ads, and blogs
Page titleOwala vs. Stanley: New Colors or New Products?
Meta descriptionOwala sells the same FreeSip in a new color every month. Stanley is building bags, coolers, and soccer collections around the Quencher. What each costs.
Web address/blog/owala-vs-stanley
Next step for the readerNotice where each brand’s ads and links send you, then answer: refresh it or build something new?
How I’ll know it workedGoogle ranking for “owala vs stanley” and time on page after 30 days
